# Data availability

The Sepsis Atlas was constructed from publicly available single-cell
RNA-sequencing datasets deposited in GEO and SRA.

The harmonized processed Atlas, frozen annotations, metadata tables,
corrected annotation references, and deconvolution signatures are available
to editors and reviewers for anonymous download during first-round peer
review.

The complete release will be deposited in the ArrayExpress collection in
BioStudies (EMBL-EBI) and will be made publicly available before any
second-round peer review and no later than publication.

The Atlas data release will be distributed under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal waiver. This license applies to the harmonized and derived Atlas
release and does not alter or supersede any rights, terms, or provenance
associated with the original source datasets.

The nine source datasets remain available from the NCBI Gene Expression
Omnibus under:

- GSE151263
- GSE163668
- GSE167363
- GSE216007
- GSE216020
- GSE217906
- GSE220189
- GSE242127
- GSE252331

Raw sequencing reads are not redistributed with this repository.
